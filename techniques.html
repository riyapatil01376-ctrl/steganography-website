Steganography Techniques

🖼️ Image Steganography

Secret information is hidden inside image data. It is one of the commonly studied forms of digital steganography.

🎵 Audio Steganography

Information is hidden inside digital audio signals.

🎬 Video Steganography

Secret information can be distributed across frames of a video.

📝 Text Steganography

Information is concealed using text formatting, characters or other textual techniques.

LSB Technique

Least Significant Bit or LSB is a commonly discussed technique for image steganography. It modifies the least significant bits of image pixel values to store information while attempting to keep visual changes small.
