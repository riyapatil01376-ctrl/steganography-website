History of Steganography

Ancient Times

Historical accounts describe methods of concealing messages so that the communication itself was not obvious.

Invisible Ink

Invisible substances were used to write messages that could later be revealed using suitable methods.

Microdots

Very small photographic reproductions were used to conceal large amounts of information.

Digital Era

Computers made it possible to hide information inside digital images, audio and video.

Modern Steganography

Today, steganography is studied as part of information security and digital communication.
